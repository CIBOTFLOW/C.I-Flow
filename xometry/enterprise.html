01Pre-sales authority

Give Sales the price, risk summary, DFM value and partner confidence needed to close.

AI AGENTS WITH GUARDRAILS

Automate preparation, not accountability.

The most useful automation removes reading, lookup and after-hours latency while leaving complex manufacturing judgment with a person.

WHAT I WOULD AUTOMATE

Preparation + narrow routing

  • Extract material, quantity, GD&T, finish and thread callouts from RFQ packages
  • Generate a one-page Part Brief for AE and Sourcing
  • Rank likely partners against the capability matrix
  • Release only highly constrained, low-ambiguity after-hours RFQs when rules are fully satisfied
  • Create a morning hit list for everything ambiguous
WHAT I WOULD NOT AUTOMATE

High-risk manufacturing decisions

  • Automatically award a job
  • Blast complex work to the entire network
  • Route exotic materials without technical review
  • Ignore undefined surface finish, tolerance or inspection requirements
  • Negotiate strategic partner economics without human ownership
Product awareness: Xometry's 2026 Workcenter and AI sourcing updates already improve job discovery and partner review. Any new automation should complement that product direction instead of creating a parallel workflow.
