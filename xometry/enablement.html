MAKE PARTNERS BETTER AT WINNING XOMETRY WORK

Use transparency, not pressure, to improve the network.

The best partner relationship is not built by sending more RFQs. It is built by sending better-fit work, giving useful win/loss feedback and helping the shop improve quoting speed, quality and capacity planning.

45-MINUTE QBR

Three conversations, not a slide marathon.

Every QBR should end with a change in routing, behavior, capability or capacity.

15 MIN · LOOK BACKPerformance

Revenue awarded, bid participation, win rate, OTD, defects, NCRs and recurring execution issues.

15 MIN · LOOK FORWARDPipeline alignment

Which jobs they win, which they lose, what to stop sending and where Xometry demand is moving.

15 MIN · IMPROVEProcess + capacity

Estimator workflow, quoting tools, machine availability, certification gaps and next-quarter capacity.

WIN / LOSS TRANSPARENCY

Tell estimators why they lost.

A marketplace becomes more valuable to partners when it teaches them how to win the next job.

PRICE

Where were they high?

Material markup, setup hours, cycle assumptions, tooling, inspection or outside processing.

FIT

Was it the wrong work?

Stop wasting estimator time on materials, tolerances, volumes or processes outside their sweet spot.

SPEED

Did timing kill the bid?

Separate quote-turnaround problems from true production-capacity problems.

Partner positioning: Xometry should feel like a zero-CAC enterprise sales channel, not a marketplace that consumes estimator hours without enough probability of winning.
WHEN TO INVOLVE OWNERSHIP

Escalate strategic decisions, not routine RFQs.

The owner should enter when the relationship economics or capability commitment changes.

MARGIN STRUCTURERate changes

Baseline shop rates, material markups or volume terms.

CAPACITYReserved hours

Dedicated spindle or cell capacity for enterprise demand.

PERFORMANCERecurring failure

Delivery or quality issues the operating team cannot resolve.

INVESTMENTCapability expansion

Certifications, specialized tooling, machines or software adoption.
