RULE OF 3-5

Curate the bid pool.

The goal is enough competition to price the job correctly without exhausting estimator bandwidth or destroying each partner's probability of winning.

CCost

Does the partner quote support a competitive customer price and Xometry's required gross-margin spread?

CConfidence

Has the shop actually delivered this material, tolerance, process and quality level successfully before?

CCapacity

Can the shop meet the requested lead time without a risk premium or schedule fragility?
