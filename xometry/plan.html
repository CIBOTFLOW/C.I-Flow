THE OPERATING PLAN

Learn the network. Improve the portfolio. Own the hardest RFQs.

The first year should progress from internal fluency and partner calibration to enterprise RFQ ownership, capability expansion and repeatable sourcing playbooks.

YEAR ONE

Four phases, each with a different job.

Do not try to redesign the network before learning how Xometry actually routes, prices and measures work.

DAYS 1-90

Learn + audit

  • Master Workcenter, RFQ workflows and internal margin logic
  • Audit assigned partner machines, materials, certifications and capacity
  • Baseline bid speed, participation, win rate, OTD and quality
  • Meet the top partner owners and estimators
  • Remove obvious quoting friction and response delays
DAYS 91-180

Standardize + coach

  • Set quoting-speed expectations by process complexity
  • Run first QBR cycle
  • Build the partner capability / preference matrix
  • Introduce DFM and cross-process conversion coaching
  • Start structured win/loss feedback with top partners
DAYS 181-270

Own + expand

  • Take lead on complex multi-process enterprise RFQs
  • Build deeper capacity in under-indexed processes
  • Use capability audits for high-risk aerospace, defense and medical work
  • Partner with AEs earlier on BOM and DFM strategy
  • Develop strategic capacity agreements where justified
DAYS 271-365

Scale + teach

  • Publish internal DFM and process-conversion guides
  • Scale the best partners into deeper production relationships
  • Formalize annual joint business planning with elite shops
  • Feed recurring portal and workflow friction to Product
  • Become the internal SME for high-value RFQ execution
