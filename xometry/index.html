WHAT THE JOB IS

The operating layer between demand and capacity.

Xometry's RFQ Marketplace handles work that needs human review: 2D drawings, assemblies, special processes and complex production requirements that do not fit a simple instant quote.

ROUTE

ACCELERATE

OPTIMIZE

Review cycle time, setup, tooling, material, finishing and process alternatives before the customer sees the price.

DEVELOP

Build better partners.

Use QBRs, win/loss feedback, capability audits and capacity planning to expand what the network can win.
