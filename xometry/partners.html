CORE PARTNERS15-20

The partners likely to carry most RFQ volume.

  • High responsiveness
  • Strong OTD and quality history
  • Broad or strategically important capacity
  • Daily estimator relationships
  • Best candidates for capacity planning
SPECIALTY + GROWTH20-40

Niche capabilities or emerging shops that should be developed into larger contributors.

  • Extrusion, die casting, stamping and tooling
  • High-end alloys and tight-tolerance work
  • ISO 13485, AS9100, ITAR, CMMC and other regulated work
  • Additive bureaus and process specialists
  • Partners being calibrated for more complex work

PARTNER DEVELOPMENT

Not every shop starts at the same trust level.

Scale responsibility only after actual delivery proves the capability.

STAGE 1Calibration

3-5 pilot jobs. Test OTD, communication, inspection documentation, packaging and DFM responsiveness.

STAGE 2Capacity allocation

Define the shop's true sweet spot, standardize rate assumptions and identify dedicated machine hours.

STAGE 3Joint business planning

Preferred RFQ routing, capability expansion, technology integration and forward capacity planning.

RELATIONSHIP MAP

Work the right person at the right cadence.

The shop owner and the estimator solve different problems.

OWNER / C-SUITE

Monthly / quarterly

Margin structure, CapEx, dedicated capacity, QBRs and strategic growth.

LEAD ESTIMATOR

Daily

RFQs, cycle assumptions, target pricing, missing drawing details and bid speed.

PLANT / PRODUCTION

Weekly

Machine availability, schedule risk, lead times and open spindle hours.

QUALITY

As needed

FAI, NCRs, CMM capability, traceability and recurring defect issues.
