HOW I THINK

Research is useful only when it changes the next commercial action.

For a sales role, I use the same underlying model I use for partnerships and business development. The objects change; the decision logic does not.

Understand the application

What does the product actually do, where does it create measurable value, and where is it a poor fit?

Prioritize accounts

Build an ICP, identify installed technology and business triggers, then turn that information into an explainable account hypothesis.

Map the route

Find decision makers, champions, technical stakeholders, partners and existing relationships that can change access or credibility.

Design the motion

Choose the wedge, economic case, partner path, proof plan and expansion path rather than sending one generic sequence everywhere.

Run the opportunity

Multi-thread the account, qualify what is real, control next steps and keep technical, commercial and executive work connected.

Learn from outcomes

Closed-won, open and closed-lost deals should continuously improve targeting, positioning, partner selection and prioritization.
