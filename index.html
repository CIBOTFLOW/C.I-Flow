02 / PROBLEMS I SOLVE

The recurring failures in business development.

Too many accounts.

Teams accumulate lists without knowing which accounts deserve human attention.

I create account tiers, signal-based prioritization and explicit next-best actions.

Technical value ≠ business value.

Buyers understand what the product does but cannot defend why they should buy it.

I translate capability into operational change, economic consequence and measurable success criteria.

Single-threaded deals.

One champion carries an opportunity while the actual decision system remains invisible.

I map the organization and deliberately build support across economic, technical and operational stakeholders.

Discovery without movement.

Calls collect information but fail to produce a stronger decision path.

I use fact finding to create value, expose consequences, test urgency and earn the next commitment.

Pilot purgatory.

Proofs of concept start without a production decision, metrics or expansion logic.

I work backward from the buying decision: success metrics, stakeholders, timeline, implementation path and expansion criteria.

CRM without intelligence.

Activity is recorded, but the system cannot explain account reality or what should happen next.

I structure account context, signals, qualification evidence, stakeholder maps and next actions so CRM becomes useful to sellers.
