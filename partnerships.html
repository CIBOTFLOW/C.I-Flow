STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIPS

I treat partnerships as commercial systems, not logo collection.

The question is not whether two companies sound complementary. It is whether the relationship changes distribution, product capability, customer value, market access, retention, implementation speed or economics enough for both sides to keep investing.

PARTNER IQ

Start with mutual leverage.

I would prioritize potential partners using the same discipline I bring to strategic accounts: fit, timing, value, access, implementation reality and a clear reason the relationship should exist now.

01

Customer overlap

Do we serve the same buyer, adjacent buyers, or different points in the same workflow where a joint motion creates more value?

02

Strategic leverage

Does the partner provide distribution, product capability, data, implementation capacity, credibility or market access we cannot efficiently create alone?

03

Mutual economics

What does each side gain—sourced revenue, influenced pipeline, retention, attach rate, product adoption, lower CAC, faster deployment or strategic access?

04

Execution probability

Can the organizations actually launch together? I look for executive sponsorship, operating owners, technical feasibility, enablement capacity and a measurable first motion.

PARTNERSHIP DESIGN

Different relationships solve different problems.

I would choose the structure after understanding the leverage—not force every relationship into the same partner program.

REFERRAL / CO-SELL

Create qualified demand together.

Shared ICP, lead ownership, attribution, qualification rules, seller enablement, registration and a defined handoff between teams.

TECHNOLOGY / INTEGRATION

Make the combined product better.

Define the customer workflow, technical dependency, product ownership, joint proof, launch plan and what adoption metric proves the integration matters.

CHANNEL / RESELLER

Extend distribution.

Partner economics, territory, enablement, certification, deal registration, support model, conflict rules and the conditions for scaling the channel.

MARKETPLACE

Reduce procurement and discovery friction.

Use the platform relationship to simplify purchasing, increase visibility and create seller-to-seller motions around existing customer commitments.

OEM / EMBEDDED

Put capability inside another product.

Align product dependencies, unit economics, support, roadmap risk, data rights and commercial incentives before integration becomes strategic debt.

STRATEGIC ALLIANCE

Coordinate around a larger market thesis.

Executive alignment, shared target accounts, product roadmap, field incentives, governance and a scorecard that can survive beyond the announcement.

HOW I WOULD RUN IT

From partner thesis to operating motion.

A signed agreement is the midpoint, not the outcome. The partnership becomes real when field teams, product teams and customers behave differently.

Value-chain map→Partner universe→Partner IQ→Mutual business case→Executive alignment→Commercial / technical design→Launch→Enablement→Co-sell→Measure→Expand
Build

Map the ecosystem, identify where value or distribution is constrained, prioritize partners, validate mutual economics and create a specific first motion.

Activate

Align owners, legal/commercial structure, technical work, field enablement, account lists, rules of engagement and launch milestones.

Scale

Measure sourced and influenced outcomes, partner engagement, time to first opportunity, attach/adoption, customer evidence and where the relationship should expand—or stop.

PARTNERSHIP LAB

Two examples of how I would think.

These are fictional examples intended to show decision logic, not claims about prior partnerships.

AI PLATFORM × CRM

Technology integration + co-sell

Thesis: the AI product becomes more valuable when embedded in the seller's existing workflow; the CRM gains differentiated AI capability and greater platform usage.

First motion: one workflow, one integration, 10–20 shared target accounts, joint success criteria, named field owners.

What I would measure: integration activation, influenced pipeline, time to first joint opportunity, workflow adoption and expansion into adjacent teams.

AI INFRASTRUCTURE × CONSULTANCY

Implementation + enterprise access

Thesis: the AI vendor gains implementation capacity and trusted enterprise access; the consultancy gains differentiated capability and new transformation work.

First motion: certify a small specialist cohort, define 3 repeatable use cases, co-develop account plans and establish deal attribution.

What I would measure: certified practitioners, registered opportunities, services attach, deployment time, production conversion and joint customer references.
