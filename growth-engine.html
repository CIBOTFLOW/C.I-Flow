AI-ENABLED GROWTH ENGINE

AI should make a seller more aware, not more robotic.

I use and build revenue systems that reduce research and administrative load while improving the quality of human judgment. The system organizes account reality. The seller still owns the relationship and the decision.

THE DIVISION OF LABOR

Automate information processing. Keep judgment human.

I do not believe the goal is an autonomous SDR that spams the market faster. The useful goal is a system that gives the human seller a better model of reality and fewer low-value tasks.

SYSTEM HANDLES

Operational load

Account enrichment, signal monitoring, research, meeting preparation, CRM hygiene, stakeholder context, qualification gaps, follow-up drafts, opportunity reminders and recommended next actions.

I HANDLE

Commercial judgment

Market choices, live conversations, discovery, value creation, political interpretation, stakeholder alignment, negotiation, closing, escalation and expansion strategy.

WHAT THE SYSTEM SHOULD LEARN

Outcomes should change the playbook.

A useful growth engine compounds commercial knowledge: which signals predicted meetings, which roles converted, where deals stalled, which value thesis held, and which expansion paths worked.

Account state→Action→Buyer response→Opportunity evidence→Outcome→Better prioritization
The point

The CRM should not just tell me what happened. It should help explain what the account means now and what I should do next.
