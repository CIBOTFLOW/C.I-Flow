06 / 07FIRST 90 DAYS
START WITH THE SYSTEM THAT EXISTS

Learn first. Standardize second. Scale third.

Start with Tulip's current customers, opportunities, team performance and funnel data before changing the system.

DAYS 0-30

Baseline the truth.

  • Meet every BDR, Frontline Sales Manager, AE, Marketing and GTM Ops partner
  • Review team tenure, past alignment models, ramp history, top performers and career goals
  • Study current customers: land use case, introductory contact, champion, economic buyer and expansion path
  • Audit current opportunities, closed-lost, rejected meetings, no-shows and recycled leads
  • Learn which titles, firmographics, signals, case studies, events and campaigns actually convert
  • Inspect current activity, AI / tooling usage, sequences, qualification and account allocation
DAYS 31-60

Standardize the architecture.

  • Agree Gold account selection with Sales and define the broader Silver universe
  • Reconcile Account IQ and Lead IQ weights to Tulip conversion history
  • Publish qualification criteria, AE handoff SLA and disposition expectations
  • Implement the hybrid vertical / regional coverage model where it improves performance
  • Assign or let BDRs choose specialist roles and launch knowledge-transfer sessions
  • Align Marketing on digital lead quality, intent, ABx and follow-up SLAs
DAYS 61-90

Scale what converts.

  • Expand the seven motions with the strongest accepted-opportunity conversion, including recycle / reactivation where timing has changed
  • Automate account enrichment, Lead IQ inputs, trigger monitoring and management reporting
  • Refine activity benchmarks only after quality guardrails hold
  • Build pod capacity, hiring and ramp plans with Sales and Finance
  • Use Sales feedback to tighten qualification and Marketing feedback to improve lead quality
  • Set quarterly targets for pipeline, account penetration, conversion and rep development
