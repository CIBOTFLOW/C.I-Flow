06 / 06FIRST 90 DAYS
START WITH THE SYSTEM THAT EXISTS

Learn first. Standardize second. Scale third.

I would not arrive with a new activity quota and a new tech stack. I would first understand which Tulip motions, people and signals already produce the best pipeline, then make those patterns easier for the whole team to execute.

DAYS 0-30

Baseline.

  • Meet every BDR, Frontline Sales Manager, AE, Marketing and GTM Ops partner
  • Review team tenure, pod alignment, ramp history and career goals
  • Audit funnel conversion, pipeline sources, rejected meetings and closed-lost patterns
  • Listen to calls and inspect current sequences, account selection and qualification
  • Map current AI/tool adoption and reporting gaps
DAYS 31-60

Standardize.

  • Agree Account IQ, Lead IQ and qualification definitions with Sales
  • Publish a simple scorecard and handoff SLA
  • Formalize coaching cadence and rep development plans
  • Tag proof, triggers and plays by industry and buyer
  • Launch a small number of measurable Gold / Silver / inbound experiments
DAYS 61-90

Scale.

  • Expand the motions with the strongest opportunity conversion
  • Automate repeatable research and reporting tasks
  • Refine pod capacity and hiring plan with Sales / Finance
  • Build team specialist guilds and cross-pod knowledge transfer
  • Set quarterly improvement targets for pipeline, conversion and rep development
STANDARDS OF EXCELLENCE

What I would hold myself accountable for.

The role succeeds when the commercial organization trusts the team, reps are improving, and pipeline becomes more predictable.

PIPELINE

Revenue commitment, not activity theater.

Forecastable opportunity creation, strong AE acceptance and clear linkage to cARR.

QUALIFICATION

One bar everywhere.

Territory tactics can change. The definition of a useful opportunity should not.

COACHING

Every rep knows the next skill.

1:1s produce specific commitments, measurable growth and credible career progression.

AI

Faster thinking, not more spam.

AI should reduce research and admin load while improving relevance, coverage and managerial insight.

ALIGNMENT

No orphaned meetings.

AEs disposition quickly, Marketing sees lead quality, and BDRs know what happened after handoff.

LEARNING

The system gets smarter.

Every quarter we know more about which accounts, buyers, triggers, messages and reps create real pipeline.

THE STANDARD

Does the system make every BDR better and pipeline more predictable?

That is the job I would be trying to do: build a top-of-funnel organization that learns quickly, develops people well, and gives Tulip's sales teams more qualified opportunities to win.
