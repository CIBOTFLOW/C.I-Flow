MARKETING VS SALES COLLABORATION

Different partners. Different questions.

Keep both loops visible: Marketing improves demand quality; Sales improves targeting and qualification.

REVENUE + DIGITAL MARKETING

Improve signal quality and lead conversion.

  • Which campaigns, keywords, AEO / SEO themes and content create qualified pipeline?
  • Which case studies, webinars, events and Tulip Library assets convert the right accounts?
  • What is the inbound follow-up SLA and how fast should BDRs respond by lead type?
  • Which intent topics and engagement patterns should increase Lead IQ?
  • How should Gold ABx, retargeting, webinars and event follow-up be coordinated?
  • Which closed-lost or inactive accounts should Marketing nurture back into an active BDR queue?
Outcome: better MQL quality, better inbound conversion, better signal definitions and more useful demand programs.
AEs + FRONTLINE SALES MANAGERS

Improve account targeting and qualification.

  • Which Gold accounts does each pod most want to win, and who are the best initial contacts?
  • Where can BDRs farm more specifically by plant, location, LOB or business unit?
  • How far down MEDDPICC / BANT should a BDR qualify before the handoff?
  • Why are accepted meetings advancing or failing after handoff?
  • How many no-shows, rejected meetings or low-quality opportunities should trigger a change in criteria?
  • Which closed-lost accounts have new timing and should return to BDR coverage?
Outcome: stronger Gold lists, clearer qualification, faster feedback and fewer orphaned meetings.
