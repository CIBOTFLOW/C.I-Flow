HOW THE TEAM GOES TO MARKET

Seven motions. One qualification bar.

The manager owns a portfolio of pipeline motions, not one outbound sequence. Each motion has a different source, level of human effort and KPI, while the definition of a qualified opportunity stays consistent.

THE MOTION PORTFOLIO

Cover the market without treating every opportunity the same.

Gold and Silver define account investment. The other motions define how demand enters the system and how the team responds.

01 · GOLDNamed strategic accounts

High-touch, multi-threaded pursuit of accounts Tulip deliberately wants to win.

Primary KPI: Gold penetration + accepted opportunities
Owner: BDR + AE + FSM + Marketing
02 · SILVERScaled fit-based outbound

1,000s to 10,000+ ICP accounts prioritized by Account IQ, intent and engagement.

Primary KPI: Silver to qualified opportunity conversion
Owner: BDR + GTM Ops
03 · INBOUNDFast response with context

Demo requests, webinars, content and product interest routed by fit and Lead IQ.

Primary KPI: MQL to held meeting to accepted SQO
Owner: Marketing + BDR
04 · INTENT SPRINTTrigger-driven pursuit

Factory expansion, FDA action, ERP change, hiring or topic surge creates a focused account sprint.

Primary KPI: trigger to meeting / opportunity conversion
Owner: BDR + GTM Ops
05 · PARTNEREcosystem-sourced pipeline

OEMs, systems integrators, technology partners and shared accounts create warmer entry paths.

Primary KPI: partner-sourced / influenced opportunities
Owner: Partner + BDR + AE
06 · EVENT / TECAccount-led field motion

Trade events, Tulip Experience Center visits and peer roundtables built around named accounts.

Primary KPI: target-account meetings + post-event SQOs
Owner: Marketing + BDR + Sales
07 · RECYCLE / REACTIVATIONRe-open good accounts when timing changes

Closed-lost, stalled, no-decision and once-engaged accounts return when a new trigger creates a better reason to talk.

Primary KPI: recycled account to accepted opportunity conversion
Owner: BDR + AE + Marketing
GOLD ACCOUNT MOTION

Named accounts get a team, not a sequence.

Gold accounts are the top strategic accounts the organization has chosen to win because the upside justifies disproportionate BDR, AE, Marketing and leadership attention.

01Account brief

Plants, systems, workflows, initiatives, buyers, relationships and likely use cases.

02Buying committee

Economic buyer, plant owner, technical buyer, champion and mutual paths.

03Human-first touches

Manual call, email and LinkedIn based on the actual plant or trigger.

04Custom asset

Vidyard, Tulip Library mockup, proof, handwritten note or Sendoso when useful.

05Field / relationship play

TEC visit, customer advocate, roundtable, executive meeting or partner path.

06Controlled scale

Once the thesis is proven, automation supports follow-up while the BDR keeps multi-threading.

Gold standard: every touch should improve the account map. The BDR is not only personalizing outreach. They are building intelligence the AE can use.
SILVER ACCOUNT MOTION

Thousands of good-fit accounts, continuously prioritized.

Silver is the broader ICP-qualified market. It is too large for bespoke account planning, so the system does the first layer of prioritization and promotes the strongest opportunities into human attention.

1Account IQ

industry · scale · geo · plants · tech

2Segment

vertical · region · use case · persona

3Sequence

relevant multi-contact outbound

4Watch engagement

intent · site behavior · replies · calls

5Promote

best accounts enter call queues or focused sprints

REP CONTROL

Personalization buckets.

BDRs decide which Silver accounts deserve more human effort based on signal quality and context.

MULTI-THREAD

Work several prospects.

Plant, Quality, Manufacturing Engineering, Operations and Digital roles can all become entry points.

NURTURE

Do not burn good fit.

Accounts without timing move to useful nurture until a stronger reason to engage appears.

07 · RECYCLE / REACTIVATION

The cheapest new opportunity may already be in Salesforce.

This is the missing motion. Closed-lost, stalled and previously engaged accounts already contain qualification history, relationships and objections. A new trigger can make them better than a brand-new cold account.

1Build recycle pool

Closed-lost, no decision, stalled, old demo, event and once-engaged accounts.

2Tag why it stopped

Timing, budget, product gap, owner change, no priority or competitor.

3Watch for change

New executive, greenfield, ERP project, FDA issue, funding, expansion or new Tulip capability.

4Choose re-entry path

Original champion, new owner, Marketing nurture, AE intro or BDR direct outreach.

5Measure recovery

Reactivated account to held meeting, accepted SQO and pipeline.

Manager value: this motion forces Sales, Marketing and BDRs to share closed-lost learning instead of treating every lost opportunity as dead forever.

HANDOFF STANDARD

A meeting is not the product.

Every motion should still produce the same quality of opportunity for the AE.

Scale, plant environment, industry and architecture make sense.

PAINNamed workflow

Paper, quality, traceability, visibility, compliance or legacy-system friction.

PERSONRelevant owner
