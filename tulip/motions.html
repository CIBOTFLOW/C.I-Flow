HOW PIPELINE GETS CREATED

One qualification bar. Multiple ways into the account.

I would centralize what "good" means, then let channel mix and personalization change based on account value, territory reality and the trigger in front of the rep.

GOLD

High-personalization named accounts.

Use where plant economics, timing and account importance justify more rep time.

  • Plant and workflow research before outreach
  • Mutual Tulip / customer relationships
  • Custom video or Tulip Library mockup teaser
  • Roundtable, TEC visit, direct mail or executive access when earned
  • Manual early touches, then controlled automation
SILVER

Scaled relevance, not generic volume.

Use signal-rich segmentation to generate enough surface area for reps to work.

  • Industry + use-case sequences
  • Multiple contacts per account
  • Engagement and buying-signal prioritization
  • Call queue built from intent and response
  • Longer nurture for accounts not ready now
INBOUND

Fast response with context.

Demo requests, webinars, case-study consumption and product interest need a clear SLA and qualification path.

  • Lead source + content consumed
  • Account fit before routing
  • Relevant use-case follow-up
  • Marketing-to-BDR SLA
  • AE disposition loop
PARTNER / EVENT

Create pipeline around trusted ecosystems.

Partners and events should be planned against accounts, not treated as separate lead buckets.

  • DMG MORI / device / SI co-prospecting
  • Tulip Experience Center visits
  • Peer engineering roundtables
  • Trade-event account lists
  • Joint follow-up ownership
TRIGGER ENGINE

Give reps reasons to call that are tied to factory change.

Signals should produce a specific workflow hypothesis and person, not simply an intent score.

Factory expansion / greenfield
press releases · permits · careers
Digital production system before paper and legacy workflows harden.
FDA 483 / audit issue
FDA · quality notices
Quality / Regulatory outreach around eBR, eDHR, traceability and documentation.
ERP / S4HANA rollout
jobs · partners · tech intent
Position Tulip as frontline execution layer connected to the enterprise system.
Manufacturing hiring
MES PM · smart factory · CI roles
New transformation owner plus evidence that the plant is actively changing.
Equipment / automation investment
capex · OEM news · plant announcements
Connect machines and operator workflows instead of creating another data island.
6sense / web intent
cMES · work instructions · eBR
Use the researched topic to choose the buyer, proof and call opening.
