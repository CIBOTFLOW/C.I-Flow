HOW THE TEAM GOES TO MARKET

Gold gets orchestration. Silver gets scale.

The qualification bar stays consistent. What changes is the amount of research, personalization, multi-threading and cross-functional investment we put behind each account.

GOLD ACCOUNT MOTION

Named accounts get a team, not a sequence.

Gold accounts are the top strategic accounts the organization has chosen to win. They can be selected because of Account IQ, AE priority, multi-site upside, relationships, timing, or strategic importance.

01Account brief

Plants, systems, workflows, current initiatives, buyers, relationships and likely use cases.

02Buying committee

Economic buyer, plant owner, technical buyer, champion and mutual paths mapped before scale outreach.

03Human-first touches

Manual call, email and LinkedIn based on the actual plant, workflow or trigger.

04Custom asset

Vidyard, Tulip Library mockup, relevant proof, handwritten note or Sendoso when it earns attention.

05Field / relationship play

TEC visit, customer advocate, roundtable, executive meeting or partner path where justified.

06Controlled scale

After the account thesis is proven, Outreach can carry touches 5-12 while the BDR continues multi-threading.

Gold standard: every touch should teach us something about the account. The BDR is not only "sending personalization." They are building an account map that the AE can use.
SILVER ACCOUNT MOTION

Thousands of good-fit accounts, continuously prioritized.

Silver can represent 1,000s to more than 10,000 accounts that meet Tulip's fit criteria. They are too broad for bespoke account planning, so the system does the first layer of prioritization.

1Account IQ

industry · scale · geo · plants · tech

2Segment

vertical · region · use case · persona

3Sequence

relevant 12-touch plays to multiple prospects

4Watch engagement

intent · opens · clicks · replies · site behavior

5Promote

best accounts move into call queues or Gold-like sprints

REP CONTROL

Personalization buckets.

BDRs can decide which Silver accounts deserve more personalization based on signal quality and context.

MULTI-THREAD

Work several prospects.

Plant, Quality, Manufacturing Engineering, Operations and Digital roles can all become entry points.

NURTURE

Do not burn good fit.

Accounts without timing can enter a longer case-study and thought-leadership nurture path instead of endless short-cycle sequences.
