EXECUTIVE SCORECARD

Seven views I would keep in front of the team.

Each view should lead to a management action, not another dashboard.

01 · PIPELINEAre we creating enough?

Accepted SQOs, sourced pipeline, cARR influence and forecast vs target.

Decision: capacity, focus, hiring
02 · MOTION MIXWhere is pipeline coming from?

Gold, Silver, Inbound, Intent, Partner, Event and Recycle contribution.

Decision: scale, fix or stop a motion
03 · GOLDAre strategic accounts opening?

Account penetration, buying committee depth, meetings and accepted opportunities.

Decision: add leadership, Marketing or AE support
04 · SILVERIs scale staying relevant?

Account IQ tier, engagement, promotion rate and qualified-opportunity conversion.

Decision: adjust segments, signals or sequences
05 · QUALIFICATIONDoes Sales trust the handoff?

Meeting held, AE acceptance, rejection reason and stage progression.

Decision: tighten coaching or criteria
06 · DEMAND QUALITYIs Marketing creating useful demand?

MQL conversion by campaign, content, event, intent source and nurture path.

Decision: improve targeting, content or SLA
07 · PEOPLEIs the team getting better?

Ramp time, skill development, specialist contribution and next-role readiness.

Decision: coach, promote, pair or hire
MANAGEMENT LOOP

Turn the scorecard into decisions.

I would keep the review simple: find the gap, identify the cause, assign the owner, and check the result.

1See the gap

conversion, coverage, quality or capacity

2Find the cause

market · message · skill · process · timing

3Choose the action

scale · fix · coach · stop

4Assign ownership

BDR · Sales · Marketing · Ops · manager

5Measure again

did the funnel actually improve?

REVIEW RHYTHM

Different problems need different clocks.

The cadence should fit Tulip's existing operating rhythm, but these are the decisions I would make at each level.

WEEKLY
Team + pipeline
Rep pacing, Gold focus, Silver signals, coaching needs, meeting quality and near-term risk.
BIWEEKLY
Sales + Marketing
Gold priorities, MQL quality, AE feedback, events, ABx and recycle opportunities.
MONTHLY
Motion review
Conversion by Gold, Silver, Inbound, Intent, Partner, Event and Recycle. Scale what works.
QUARTERLY
Capacity + talent
Quota, headcount, territories, hiring, promotion readiness, specialist coverage and career movement.
STANDARDS

What good looks like.

The standard is not more activity. It is a team that learns faster and produces more trusted pipeline.

GOLD

Named accounts have real plans.

Buying committee, relationships, plant context, trigger, proof and coordinated actions are visible.

SILVER

Scale stays relevant.

Account IQ, Lead IQ and signals continuously surface the best accounts for rep attention.

QUALIFICATION

One bar everywhere.

Territory tactics can change. The definition of a useful opportunity should not.

COACHING

Every rep knows the next skill.

1:1s, specialist roles and team-led training create measurable improvement.

ALIGNMENT

No orphaned leads or meetings.

Sales dispositions quickly, Marketing sees lead quality, and BDRs know what happened after handoff.

LEARNING

The system gets smarter.

Every quarter we know more about which accounts, buyers, signals and motions create real pipeline.
