VERTICAL MAP

Match the operating problem to the industry.

The same platform should sound different to a Quality Director in pharma than to a Manufacturing VP in aerospace.

Life ScienceseBR · eDHR · deviations · logbooks

Metrics: batch release, first-pass yield, deviation rate, compliance effort. Buyers: Manufacturing, Quality, Digital, IT.

Aerospace & Defenseguided assembly · traceability · FAI · tooling

Metrics: scrap, rework, audit readiness, engineering-change response. Buyers: Manufacturing, Quality, Programs, OT/IT.

High-Tech / Electronicsvision · work instructions · skills · quality

Metrics: takt time, operator ramp, defect rate, throughput. Buyers: Ops, Manufacturing Engineering, CI.

Industrial / AutomotiveOEE · quality · changeover · material flow

Metrics: downtime, yield, inspection time, WIP, labor efficiency. Buyers: Plant, Ops, CI, Manufacturing Engineering.
