THE MARKET ARCHITECTURE

Decide who deserves human attention before deciding how to reach them.

Define the named strategic market, the broader fit-based market, and when an account or person earns more attention.

GOLD + SILVER

Two account universes. Two levels of investment.

Gold is a strategic choice. Silver is a scaled fit pool. Account IQ informs both.

GOLD ACCOUNTSNamed strategic accounts

A limited list the organization deliberately wants to penetrate because the upside justifies disproportionate BDR, AE, Marketing and leadership attention.

How an account gets here

  • High fit and multi-site expansion potential
  • Strategic logo, vertical or geographic importance
  • AE / Frontline Sales Manager nomination
  • Known relationship, customer adjacency or partner path
  • Active initiative, event, trigger or executive priority
≠different levels
of investment
SILVER ACCOUNTS1,000s to 10,000+ fit accounts

The broader ICP-qualified market. Accounts meet fit criteria but earn more human attention only when signals or Sales priority justify it.

How the team works it

  • Account IQ and industry segmentation
  • Automated enrichment and trigger monitoring
  • Relevant industry / use-case sequences
  • Multiple contacts per account
  • Intent and engagement move the best accounts into rep call queues
Gold and Silver are different account pools. Gold is the named strategic list chosen with Sales leadership. Silver is the broader fit-based market. A Silver account can receive Gold-level attention when timing, intent, a relationship or Sales priority makes it worth deeper investment, without automatically changing tiers.
VERTICAL MAP

Match the operating problem to the industry.

The same platform should sound different to a Quality Director in pharma than to a Manufacturing VP in aerospace.

Life ScienceseBR · eDHR · deviations · logbooks

Metrics: batch release, first-pass yield, deviation rate, compliance effort. Buyers: Manufacturing, Quality, Digital, IT.

Aerospace & Defenseguided assembly · traceability · FAI · tooling

Metrics: scrap, rework, audit readiness, engineering-change response. Buyers: Manufacturing, Quality, Programs, OT/IT.

High-Tech / Electronicsvision · work instructions · skills · quality

Metrics: takt time, operator ramp, defect rate, throughput. Buyers: Ops, Manufacturing Engineering, CI.

Industrial / AutomotiveOEE · quality · changeover · material flow

Metrics: downtime, yield, inspection time, WIP, labor efficiency. Buyers: Plant, Ops, CI, Manufacturing Engineering.
