APPLY THE THESIS

Use JVs only where shared ownership creates real strategic leverage.

The strongest JV opportunities are the ones where Blue Origin contributes unique flight or infrastructure capability and the partner contributes equally difficult industrial IP, capital, operating expertise or distribution.

EXECUTIVE DECISION MEMO · CRYOGENIC INFRASTRUCTURE

Recommendation: do not form the JV yet.

The attractive end-state may be a shared orbital-refueling business, but the current decision should be whether to spend limited money and management attention proving the thesis.

DECISIONAuthorize a gated feasibility workstream

12-16 week technical / commercial study with one or two industrial-gas counterparties. No new entity and no broad exclusivity.

WHY NOWMobility infrastructure is becoming real

Blue Ring, lunar systems and government missions increase the strategic value of cryogenic storage, transfer and servicing capabilities.

WHAT MUST BE TRUEDemand before infrastructure

At least one credible anchor demand source, a technically plausible architecture and a capital plan that does not leave Blue carrying most of the downside.

STOP CONDITIONSKill it early if the gates fail

No anchor demand, unacceptable boil-off / transfer performance, partner unwilling to fund, or economics inferior to a normal supplier / service contract.

GATE 1Feasibility

Architecture, partner capability, export / regulatory review, rough-order economics.

GATE 2Co-development

Funded technical milestones, test plan, foreground IP and anchor-customer development.

GATE 3Entity decision

Only form JV/SPV if shared asset ownership, outside capital and recurring service economics justify it.

JV GATE

Four questions before creating a separate entity.

A JV adds governance, tax, accounting and exit complexity. It should exist only when the asset and economics justify that burden.

01 · UNIQUE CONTRIBUTIONS

Does each side bring something hard to replicate?

Capital alone is rarely enough. The strongest structures combine differentiated IP, access, operating expertise and customer reach.

02 · DURABLE MARKET

Is there a real business after the first mission?

Look for recurring demand, anchor customers, repeat utilization and a path beyond government demonstration funding.

03 · GOVERNANCE

Can control be designed cleanly?

Board rights, reserved matters, funding obligations, deadlock, IP and exit must be workable before launch.

04 · BETTER THAN CONTRACT

Why not just sign an MSA?

If a normal commercial agreement can achieve the objective with less risk, use it.
