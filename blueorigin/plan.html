THE OPERATING PLAN

Learn the portfolio fast. Originate real deals. Build the system while executing.

The first 90 days should produce better market intelligence, reusable deal infrastructure, executive-ready partnership theses and meaningful progress on live opportunities, not just onboarding.

90-DAY SPRINT

Three phases with visible outputs.

Exact deal milestones depend on the inherited pipeline, but the operating standard should be aggressive.

DAYS 1-30 · LEARN + MAP

Understand where Blue Origin needs external leverage.

  • Meet Corporate Development, BU leadership, New Glenn, In-Space Systems, Lunar, LEO, Engines and Exploration Systems
  • Meet Legal, Finance / FP&A, Tax, Strategy, Engineering, Sales, Supply Chain, IT/Security and HR
  • Audit active partnerships, abandoned deals, minority investments, partner inquiries and strategic pipeline
  • Review historical term sheets, approvals, diligence patterns and common negotiation bottlenecks
  • Map key Blue Origin IP and capability surfaces that can support partnership monetization
  • Build the first Partner IQ landscape across public relationships and new target categories
DAYS 31-60 · BUILD + ORIGINATE

Create the reusable corporate development engine.

  • Launch market maps for cryogenic infrastructure, microgravity R&D, lunar industry, connectivity and hosted payloads
  • Stand up intent signals across filings, funding, procurement, product announcements and strategic hiring
  • Create reusable partnership memo, term sheet, JV/SPV outline, diligence tracker and risk register templates
  • Build partnership portfolio reporting with pipeline stage, upside, risk, capital, knowledge gaps and next action
  • Develop 3-5 executive-ready partnership theses with partner shortlists and structure recommendations
  • Begin external discovery on the highest-priority theses where internal sponsors approve
DAYS 61-90 · EXECUTE + PROVE

Turn the framework into live deal progress.

  • Lead at least one live strategic-partnership workstream end to end
  • Prepare an executive-ready recommendation with economics, risk and structure alternatives
  • Advance one opportunity to a meaningful term-sheet, MOU or internal-approval milestone if the inherited pipeline permits
  • Launch qualified external conversations across at least two priority partnership categories
  • Identify one underutilized Blue Origin capability with credible external monetization potential
  • Present a 12-month partnership roadmap with portfolio gaps, top targets and required executive decisions
Day-90 bar: senior leaders should already trust the role to frame a complex partnership, coordinate the cross-functional diligence team and present a concise decision recommendation.
