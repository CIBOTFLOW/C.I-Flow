DILIGENCE WORKSTREAMS

Every function answers a different failure mode.

The job description explicitly places cross-functional diligence leadership in this role.

LEGALCan we sign it safely?

Entity structure, IP, liability, change of control, export control, exclusivity, termination.

FINANCE + ACCOUNTINGDoes value exceed capital and risk?

NPV, cash timing, milestone economics, valuation, revenue recognition, downside cases.

TAXWhere should value sit?

Entity jurisdiction, cross-border flows, tax attributes, transfer pricing and SPV consequences.

ENGINEERINGCan the product actually support it?

TRL, interfaces, qualification burden, custom engineering and program schedule impact.

OPERATIONS + SUPPLY CHAINCan we deliver at scale?

Capacity, sourcing, bottlenecks, production conflicts, logistics and recurring service load.

IT + SECURITYCan systems and data interoperate?

Cyber, data-sharing, system integration, cloud, access control and operational resilience.

SALES / BUWill a customer pay?

Anchor demand, pricing, alternatives, pipeline, channel conflict and commercial ownership.

HR + ORGANIZATIONCan the team execute?

Key talent, secondments, leadership model, incentive alignment and integration load.

STRATEGYShould Blue Origin own this?

Portfolio fit, strategic optionality, make-vs-partner logic and opportunity cost.
