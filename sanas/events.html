If the BPO wins, Sanas lands inside the new seats / program.

WHY THIS MATTERS

The Growth AE is not limited to chasing people already inside a deployment. A BPO's own revenue events can create the Sanas expansion opportunity.

SIGNALS THAT CHANGE PRIORITY

Every signal must point to an account action.

HIRING

“Neutral accent” / bilingual / major site ramp.

Find the LOB, site leader and BPO Account Director. Ask whether Sanas can widen the talent pool or standardize the launch.

TECH STACK

Genesys, Five9, NICE or Talkdesk change.

A voice-stack migration can be a natural moment to review the speech layer because Sanas publicly positions itself alongside these platforms.

EXEC CHANGE

New VP CX / Ops / Vendor Management.

Show proof already inside their company and give them a fast, low-risk expansion question to evaluate.

M&A / GEO

New region or acquired workforce.

Consistency becomes an operational problem. Map the new teams and determine whether an existing deployment can become the standard.

CX PAIN

Repeated public complaints about clarity or support.

Use the signal as a discovery hypothesis—not as a gotcha. Validate internally and with the customer.

PRODUCT RELEASE

New Sanas capability.

Speech Intelligence, Language Translation and Speech Enhancement create legitimate reasons to reopen trusted accounts with a new workflow.
