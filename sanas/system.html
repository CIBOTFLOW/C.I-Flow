THE ACCOUNT BRIEF

Every priority account should answer these questions on one screen.

01 · CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION

Where is Sanas live?

Product · site · LOB · program · seats/users · adoption · baseline → outcome · renewal / QBR.

02 · WHITESPACE

What exists around it?

Other LOBs · delivery locations · BPO client programs · adjacent products · estimated addressable teams.

03 · PEOPLE

Who can move it?

Champion · economic buyer · decision maker · site leader · BPO Account Director · CSM · mutual relationship.

04 · TIMING

Why now?

QBR · budget · hiring · executive change · CCaaS migration · M&A · RFP / renewal · peak season · event.

OUTREACH

Less volume. More reason.

What the message contains.

1 · ContextWhy I am contacting this person specifically.
2 · ProofOne result from their company, BPO or a genuinely similar workflow.
3 · HypothesisThe specific adjacent LOB / site / client program / product I think is worth testing.
4 · TimingThe real reason the conversation is useful now.
5 · Ask10–30 minutes to validate the hypothesis and decide whether to involve the account team.
CADENCE10–12

Touches per executive before resting the contact for a quarter, using call + email + LinkedIn. My research notes suggest an initial target of roughly 10–30 high-value calls / emails / LI messages per week; I would calibrate this to Sanas's actual funnel conversion and monthly pipeline target.
