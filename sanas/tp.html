THREE PLAYS I WOULD TEST

Specific hypotheses, each with a different door.

PLAY 01 · CLIENT PROGRAM

Take proven TP metrics into a similar enterprise queue.

  • Identify TP Account Directors with large U.S.-facing offshore programs.
  • Prioritize programs where CSAT/AHT/FCR are contract metrics.
  • Use the TP proof before asking the enterprise for a new technical evaluation.
MEETING OBJECTIVE

Validate the target queue, baseline KPIs, seats and whether the BPO wants Sanas positioned in the next client review.

PLAY 02 · GEOGRAPHY

Take India / Philippines proof into LATAM.

  • Map TP client programs operating in Colombia, Mexico and other nearshore markets.
  • Look for English-speaking hiring, new facilities, client migrations and SLA pressure.
  • Use the same-client-program path when possible.
MEETING OBJECTIVE

Choose one LATAM program where clarity or multilingual support can be measured against an existing Sanas-enabled site.

PLAY 03 · NEW PRODUCT

Cross-sell inside a relationship that already passed security.

  • Map programs using Accent Translation but not another Sanas product.
  • Match product to problem: language, degraded audio, live QA/compliance.
  • Bring Product / SE only after a specific workflow is identified.
MEETING OBJECTIVE

Define one adjacent use case and the smallest credible evaluation path.
