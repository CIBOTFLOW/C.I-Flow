MEETING OBJECTIVE

Get Sanas included in one client review as a performance or differentiation lever.

Where one platform module has not solved the whole workflow.

Multi-product architecture creates another growth surface inside already-approved environments.

  • Poor audio / open floor → Speech Enhancement.

Choose one client workflow where an adjacent module has a measurable business case.
