PARTNERS

Pitch the partner’s win first.

They do not need another vendor pitch. They need a reason to spend seller time, credibility or delivery resources on the opportunity.

Hyperscaler

Help the customer move on AI.

Bring a useful workload, a credible use case and a clean co-sell path.

“I think there’s a real use case in this account. Can we spend 10 minutes deciding whether it helps your customer and your AI/cloud objective?”
GSI / SI

Create services around the platform.

Architecture, integration, governance, process design, change and managed services.

“I’m not asking you to carry another logo. I’m looking for problems where Kore.ai creates the platform opportunity and your team owns the transformation around it.”
CX / BPO

Improve the delivery model.

Use AI to protect the customer relationship and move toward higher-value managed outcomes.

Regional / Sovereign

Pair platform with local trust.

Local market access, compliance and delivery can be the reason the joint motion works.

HOW I’D PRIORITIZE A PARTNER

Useful beats famous.

Access

Can they open doors?

Real account relationships.

Economics

Do they benefit?

Cloud, services, retention or differentiation.

Action

Will sellers engage?

Not just executive-level agreement.

Proof

Does it create pipeline?

Meetings → opportunities → wins.

The best partner relationship is one where both sellers know which account to work next.
