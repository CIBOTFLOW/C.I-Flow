HOW I’D SELL

One account. One problem. One reason to work together.

The partner motion only matters if it improves the customer deal.

Hyperscaler co-sell
When

Active AWS/Azure account + real AI workload + Kore.ai use case.

Pitch

“I think this account has a real service/workflow problem. Does it line up with what your team is trying to move?”

Close

Account owner + joint discovery + marketplace/co-sell path if useful.

GSI services attach
When

Integration, process, governance or change work is substantial.

Pitch

Kore.ai creates the platform opportunity; the GSI owns valuable transformation work around it.

Close

Joint scope + roles + executive sponsor + customer meeting.

Partner whitespace
When

Partner already has trust in a high-IQ account.

Pitch

Show the specific use case and Kore.ai proof that looks like their client.

Close

Warm intro to the business owner and discovery.

Stalled-deal rescue
When

Good use case is blocked by access, implementation, procurement or confidence.

Pitch

Bring the partner in only if they can change the reason the deal is stuck.

Close

Remove the blocker—or walk and spend time elsewhere.
