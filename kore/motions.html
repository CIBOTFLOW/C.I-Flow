SALES MOTIONS

Four motions I’d actually run.

Each starts with a customer problem and a reason for the partner to participate.

1. Hyperscaler co-sell
When

Customer has an active cloud / AI initiative and Kore.ai fits a real workflow.

Pitch

Customer outcome + useful AI workload + joint path to procurement.

Close

Named account owners, customer meeting and agreed next step.

2. GSI services attach
When

The customer needs integration, architecture, governance or change work.

Pitch

Kore.ai creates the platform opportunity; the GSI monetizes the transformation.

Close

Joint scope, roles, sponsor and commercial path.

3. Installed-base / whitespace
When

A partner already has trust in an account where Kore.ai has strong use-case fit.

Pitch

One account. One problem. One reason to bring Kore.ai in.

Close

Warm introduction and discovery with the business owner.

4. Stalled-deal rescue
When

A good use case is stuck on access, implementation, procurement or confidence.

Pitch

Bring in the partner only if they can change the reason the deal stalled.

Close

Remove the blocker or stop spending time.
