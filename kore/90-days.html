FIRST 90 DAYS

Learn fast. Create pipeline. Prove a repeatable motion.

I’d align exact targets with the manager. My first job would be to learn what already works before trying to change anything.

DAYS 1–30

Learn

Understand the products, partner landscape, active pipeline and sales process.

  • Review wins, losses and stalled deals
  • Meet priority partner and Kore.ai sellers
  • Learn CRM / forecasting expectations
  • Identify 10–20 accounts worth studying deeply
DAYS 31–60

Create

Turn what I learned into focused partner/account motions.

  • Run short partner account-mapping sessions
  • Open customer discovery conversations
  • Test hyperscaler and GSI pitches
  • Create first qualified partner-sourced opportunities
DAYS 61–90

Prove

Show which motions deserve more investment.

  • Progress early opportunities
  • Repeat what is producing meetings and pipeline
  • Run one focused joint campaign / executive motion
  • Give clear pipeline, risk and next-step visibility
THE DASHBOARD I’D CARE ABOUT

Just enough to know whether I’m doing the job.

1Partner-sourced pipelineQualified, not merely registered.
2Partner-influenced pipelineWhere the relationship materially changes the deal.
3ProgressionMeetings → discovery → next step → commercial action.
4Revenue impactBookings / wins attributable to the motion.

By day 90, I’d want the team to know two things: which partner motions I can own, and which ones are producing real pipeline.
