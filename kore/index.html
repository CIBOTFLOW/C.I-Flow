Deal evidence hierarchy

Closed won: highest weight. What application, account profile, partner role, geography, services attach and deal path repeated?
Open: medium weight. Where is the partner actively helping create or advance pipeline?
Closed lost: diagnostic weight. Did the relationship fail on fit, competition, access, delivery, procurement, timing or execution?
No deal evidence: do not let brand size substitute for actual partnership productivity.

05 · PARTNERSHIP DEVELOPMENT & SALES

Build reusable plays instead of generic partner outreach.

Each play should define the trigger, value for each party, target personas, proof, next step and measurement plan.

HYPERSCALER

Cloud workload / committed-spend motion

Find accounts where a Kore.ai workload advances AWS or Microsoft AI objectives and where approved marketplace or co-sell paths can simplify procurement. Lead with customer value and workload relevance—not “quota burn” language.

GSI

Services attach

Show the surrounding implementation opportunity: process mapping, architecture, integration, data, security, testing, governance, change management and managed services.

INSTALLED BASE

Partner whitespace mining

Cross partner-served accounts against Kore.ai ICP + AI IQ to find high-fit clients where the partner already has trust or delivery ownership.

TRIANGLE

Hyperscaler + GSI + Kore.ai

Customer gets platform and delivery; GSI gets services; hyperscaler gets workload; Kore.ai gets a credible route to deployment and expansion.

RECOVERY

Stalled / orphaned opportunity

Re-open deals where a partner can change the loss condition: executive access, implementation capacity, procurement route, cloud alignment, local delivery or trust.

GOVERNANCE

Agent-sprawl wedge

When customers already have agents across frameworks, lead with governance, policy, observability and management rather than forcing a replacement discussion.

BPO / CX

Human + AI service transformation

Frame automation as a way for service providers to improve delivery economics and create higher-value AI-enabled managed services, not simply eliminate labor.

SOVEREIGN / REGIONAL

Localized agentic transformation

Pair Kore.ai platform capabilities with local infrastructure, compliance, language, industry expertise and delivery relationships.

PARTNER-TO-PARTNER

Broker the ecosystem

Connect the GSI account partner, hyperscaler AE and Kore.ai account team when all three have a rational economic role in the same customer transformation.

06 · COMPETITION

Competitive intelligence should improve allocation.

The goal is not to make Kore.ai “win” every row. It is to understand when to stay, coexist, displace or stop spending time.

Stay

Application, architecture, economics and partner route fit Kore.ai naturally. Invest.

Coexist

An incumbent can remain while Kore.ai fills orchestration, governance, service or workflow gaps.

Displace

A documented limitation creates a credible replacement business case and the switching cost is justified.

Walk

Another platform is structurally better suited or politically entrenched. Preserve resources for a stronger pursuit.

Competitive footprint to map inside top accounts

NATIVE PLATFORM

Cloud / suite agents

Microsoft, AWS, Google, Salesforce, ServiceNow and other strategic platforms that may compete, coexist or create a partner route.

SERVICE / CONTACT CENTER

CCaaS + CX automation

Identify incumbent contact-center, virtual-agent and agent-assist layers and whether Kore.ai integrates, augments or replaces them.

AGENT FRAMEWORKS

Open / custom agent stacks

Agent frameworks may be a build alternative for Artemis but can also create an AMP governance opportunity when enterprises operate heterogeneous agent estates.

Attribution types

10 · SOCIAL + FIELD DEVELOPMENT

Build partner mindshare around specific account motions.

Content and events should support account and partner strategy rather than become activity for its own sake.

Account-based social

Translate Kore.ai customer stories, product releases and use cases into role-specific content for hyperscaler sellers, GSI account partners and enterprise AI leaders.

Monthly lunches

Small Bay Area partner sessions around one topic or account cluster. Track invitations → attendance → follow-up meetings → opportunities → pipeline.

Quarterly dinners
