ACCOUNT INTELLIGENCE

Three questions before an account becomes a pursuit.

Separate the company fit, the AI opportunity and the route into the account instead of hiding all three inside one score.

ACCOUNT IQ

Should Kore.ai want this company?

Industry, size, service volume, regulation, digital complexity, transformation activity and enterprise buying potential.

AI IQ

Where can Kore.ai create value?

Current AI use cases, agent estate, cloud, CRM, ITSM, contact-center and data stack; map plausible applications and legacy integration opportunities.

PARTNER ROUTE IQ

Who changes our access or economics?

Hyperscalers, GSIs, BPOs, implementation partners and relationship paths ranked by evidence of account access and relevance.

PARTNER IQ

Measure a partner by what the relationship can produce.

Closed-won evidence should carry the most weight. Open opportunities show current motion. Closed-lost deals explain where the partnership or product fit breaks.

Relationship evidence

Won, open and lost opportunities; revenue; industries; geographies; account overlap; relationship owners; executive sponsorship and certifications.

Services + economic fit

What the partner can monetize around Kore.ai: architecture, integration, process mapping, security, governance, change management, deployment and managed services.

Whitespace

Partner-served accounts that fit Kore.ai's ICP but do not show an identified Kore.ai deployment. Rank the overlap into a joint account plan.

CO-SELL PLAYS

Turn relationships into repeatable motions.

The site should give the partnership manager a play to run—not just a partner profile to read.

HYPERSCALER

Committed-spend / workload motion

Identify where a Kore.ai workload advances the hyperscaler's AI objectives and can use approved marketplace or co-sell mechanisms.

GSI

Services attach

Lead with the implementation and transformation work the GSI can monetize around the platform, not simply another software logo.

INSTALLED BASE

Partner whitespace mining

Find partner customers with strong Kore.ai application fit, then map the partner relationship owner and internal account team.

TRIANGLE

GSI + hyperscaler + Kore.ai

Customer gets technology and delivery; GSI gets services; hyperscaler gets workload; Kore.ai gets a credible route to production.

RECOVERY

Orphaned / stalled opportunity

Ask whether a partner can change the reason a deal stalled: implementation capacity, access, procurement, cloud alignment or executive credibility.

GOVERNANCE

Agent-sprawl wedge

Where an enterprise already has agents across frameworks, lead with governance and observability rather than forcing a replacement conversation.

COMPETITION

Know when to stay, partner, displace—or walk.

Competitive intelligence should improve resource allocation, not just produce battlecards.

Stay

The use case and architecture fit Kore.ai naturally.

Partner

The incumbent can coexist and create a broader solution.

Displace

A concrete weakness creates a credible replacement motion.

Walk

The competitor is structurally better suited; preserve time for higher-probability pursuits.

END STATE

The dashboard should answer one question every morning.

“Where can I create the most valuable partner-assisted commercial action today, and what evidence supports that decision?”
