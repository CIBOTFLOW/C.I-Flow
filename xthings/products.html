DIFFERENTIATION

Open architecture is a commercial lever.

The strongest differentiation is not “more hardware.” It is the ability to connect physical systems without forcing every customer into one narrow deployment model.

INTEROPERABILITYOpen protocols reduce lock-in fear

Matter, OSDP, ONVIF, Zigbee, Z-Wave, Wi-Fi and other supported standards widen integration options. Always verify device-level compatibility before promising reuse.

DEPLOYMENT FLEXIBILITYCloud, local or hybrid

The architecture can be positioned around the buyer's latency, privacy, resilience and operations requirements rather than one mandated topology.

COMMERCIAL DISCIPLINE

What I would do and what I would not do.

IoT deals become expensive quickly when sales gets ahead of technical reality. The BDM should accelerate deals without creating implementation debt.

DO
  • Start discovery from the customer's current stack, protocols and workflows.
  • Sell the operational outcome: fewer systems, faster access workflows, local resilience, lower integration friction.
  • Bring Product / Solutions Engineering into complex API, biometric and legacy-integration questions early.
  • Use a time-boxed pilot with written technical and commercial success criteria.
  • Package hardware and recurring cloud value together where the use case supports it.
DO NOT
  • Promise “no rip-and-replace” until the exact reader, controller, camera, wiring and protocol path is verified.
  • Claim biometric compliance is automatic. Legal and privacy requirements vary by jurisdiction and deployment.
  • Invent partner margin or SaaS revenue-share percentages before Finance and channel policy approve them.
  • Turn every enterprise request into custom engineering before the economic buyer and commercial value are real.
  • Infer quota from OTE. Confirm revenue credit, margin model and compensation mechanics first.
