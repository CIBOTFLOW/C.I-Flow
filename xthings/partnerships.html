WHO · HOW · WHEN

A partner is valuable when it creates access, implementation leverage, or recurring demand.

The partner strategy should optimize for sourced revenue and deployment leverage. Different partner types enter the deal at different moments, so enablement, economics and deal registration should reflect the motion they actually own.

PARTNER LANDSCAPE

Five partner motions, each with a different job.

Xthings already invites distributors, installers, system integrators, OEM/ODM partners, technology partners, developers and enterprise buyers into its ecosystem. The BD opportunity is to make those routes measurable and productive.

SYSTEMS INTEGRATORS

Install + influence

Low-voltage and security firms that own the field relationship and can specify Xthings across multiple end customers.

WHEN: retrofit, access-control bid, new commercial deployment
MSPs / IT PARTNERS

Connect physical + digital identity

IT firms that already manage directories, networks, SaaS and user lifecycle for business clients.

WHEN: HR/identity integration, office standardization, multi-site IT project
A&E / SPECIFIERS

Win before the bid

Architects, consultants and engineers that influence access, security and smart-building specifications.

WHEN: schematic design, Division 08/28, new build or major renovation
PROPERTY / DEVELOPERS

Standardize portfolios

Developers and operators that can deploy smart access, video and cloud management across buildings and units.

WHEN: development, renovation, portfolio technology refresh
TECH / OEM / PLATFORM

Expand the ecosystem

Cloud, identity, property, automation and device partners that can integrate through APIs, SDKs and open protocols.

WHEN: joint customer demand or repeatable integration use case

PARTNER FLYWHEEL

Turn one project into a reusable route to market.

The best ecosystem is not five separate referral programs. It is a chain where early influence, technical validation, installation and recurring software economics reinforce each other.

1 · TRIGGERProject appears

New build, retrofit, lease, portfolio refresh or security modernization.

2 · SPECIFYA&E / consultant

Design Xthings-compatible access, security and open integration into the project.

3 · VALIDATEIntegrator / MSP

Confirm topology, protocols, install scope, API requirements and pilot plan.

4 · DEPLOYEnd customer

Hardware + software + implementation with clear ownership and support boundaries.

5 · EXPANDRegister next deal

Use proof, installed base and recurring revenue to source the next project.

PARTNER PROGRAM DESIGN

Economics should reward behavior Xthings actually wants.

I would not invent margin tiers or SaaS splits from outside the company. The program should be designed from gross margin, support cost, sourced pipeline and the work the partner agrees to own.

AUTHORIZED

Install / resell

Technical onboarding, standard pricing, deal registration and access to sales assets. Best for partners proving first deployments.

CERTIFIED

Source + co-sell

Higher enablement, stronger economics, qualified lead sharing and co-marketing tied to training and active pipeline.

STRATEGIC

Repeat revenue engine

Joint account planning, roadmap feedback, deeper technical access and executive cadence for partners producing material recurring business.

Channel conflict rule: protect registered opportunities, define direct-sales exceptions, set consistent pricing governance, and never surprise a partner by undercutting them in an account they sourced.
