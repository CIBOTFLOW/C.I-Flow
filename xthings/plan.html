OPERATING PLAN

Learn the stack fast, prove revenue motions locally, then scale the ones that work.

Fremont should be the laboratory for the North American playbook. The first 90 days should establish technical credibility, partner and account baselines, and live commercial evidence. Year one should scale only the channels and ICPs that produce healthy revenue.

90-DAY SPRINT

Three phases with founder-visible outputs.

The goal is not a 90-day strategy project. It is to learn while selling and leave each phase with measurable pipeline, partner and product feedback.

DAYS 1-30 · LEARN + BASELINE

Become technically credible and map reality.

  • Provision Xthings One / Station and personally understand core commercial device flows
  • Shadow Product, Solutions, Marketing, Sales and Operations on live opportunities
  • Learn current pricing, margins, partner program, support boundaries and implementation constraints
  • Audit existing partners by sourced revenue, activation and opportunity history
  • Audit Closed Lost, stalled and nurture opportunities for reactivation
  • Map first 30 high-potential integrators / MSPs and priority direct accounts in the Bay Area
  • Define first version of Partner IQ / Account IQ using real historical evidence
DAYS 31-60 · ENGAGE + VALIDATE

Run the motions against live buyers.

  • Launch targeted partner and direct-account outreach around active triggers
  • Run joint account planning with the strongest existing partners
  • Stand up a clean deal-registration and next-action discipline in CRM
  • Launch first structured pilots with defined success criteria and decision dates
  • Create product feedback cases for repeated API / integration blockers tied to revenue
  • Test inbound routing for partner applications, developer interest and enterprise demos
DAYS 61-90 · CLOSE + CHOOSE

Prove what deserves scale.

  • Close the first partner-led and direct opportunities supported by real pipeline timing
  • Compare partner types and ICPs by conversion, cycle time, margin and support burden
  • Identify the best 2-3 repeatable GTM motions for North America
  • Turn first wins into reference, co-sell and expansion plans
  • Present founder review: what converts, what stalls, product gaps, channel conflicts and next 12-month priorities

DAY-30 EXECUTIVE OUTPUTS

Each leader should get something decision-ready.

The first month should not end with a strategy presentation. It should end with operating information leadership can use immediately.

CEOWhere should we place the next commercial bets?

Partner productivity baseline, best ICP evidence, pipeline coverage, deal economics and the 2-3 motions worth funding harder.

STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIPSWhich partners deserve attention?

Current partner segmentation, activation gaps, target shortlist, co-sell opportunities and channel-conflict issues.

PRODUCT MARKETINGWhat proof is missing from the field?

Top objections, integration questions, competitive gaps, pilot requirements and the sales assets most likely to improve conversion.

BASELINE FIRST

Do not reverse-engineer quota from the compensation line.

The posted OTE is aggressive, but quota and revenue credit cannot be inferred responsibly without the comp plan. By Day 30 I would establish the commercial facts and set targets with leadership.

REVENUE CREDITWhat counts?

Hardware revenue, gross margin, software ARR, partner-sourced revenue, multi-year TCV and renewals may be credited differently.

DEAL ECONOMICSWhat is a good deal?

ACV / TCV, hardware margin, software attach, discounting, integration effort and support burden.

PARTNER BASELINEWho is actually productive?

Signed, trained, active, sourcing, co-selling, closing and expanding are different states.

SALES BASELINEWhere does conversion break?

Source to meeting, qualification, pilot, proposal, security / legal review and close.

ICP BASELINEWhere do we win?

Vertical, deployment type, incumbent technology, protocol environment and buyer role.

CAPACITYHow fast can we responsibly scale?

Solutions engineering, onboarding, inventory, field support and product roadmap constraints.
