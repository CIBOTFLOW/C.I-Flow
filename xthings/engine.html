THE ENGINE

Signals become useful only when they create a better next action.

The Xthings version should combine external project signals, product / partner intent and CRM evidence without automating outreach blindly.

1Collect

Permits, construction, company growth, partner applications, product intent and ecosystem signals.

2Match

Resolve company, property, project, partner and contact identities into one commercial record.

3Enrich

Current stack, project type, incumbent, decision roles, protocols, timing and evidence quality.

4Score

Partner IQ, Account IQ, Lead IQ and confidence remain separate so weak evidence cannot masquerade as high fit.

5Brief

Why now, value hypothesis, technical fit, open questions and recommended next action.

6Act + learn

Human-approved outreach, partner motion, CRM update and win/loss feedback into the model.
