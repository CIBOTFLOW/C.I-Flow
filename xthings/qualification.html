QUALIFY FOR ECONOMICS AND TECHNICAL FIT

Not every connected-space opportunity deserves enterprise BD time.

Use fast BANT filters to confirm commercial reality, then MEDDPICC for complex deals. The IQ stack ranks where to spend attention, but actual win/loss data should continuously recalibrate the weights.

THREE CORE ICP MOTIONS

Partner leverage, property scale and high-consequence facilities.

The attached research points to three especially useful initial motions. I would validate them against Xthings' current gross margin, attach rate, support burden and close history before declaring a permanent ICP hierarchy.

ICP A · CHANNEL

Systems integrators + MSPs

Security installers, low-voltage contractors and IT firms serving commercial clients.

  • Need: modern cloud / edge capabilities without losing implementation margin
  • Authority: Owner, VP Operations, Procurement, Solutions Engineering
  • Trigger: retrofit bid, new client requirement, legacy displacement
ICP B · PROPERTY

Multifamily + commercial property

Developers, operators and property teams managing access, turnover and multi-site operations.

  • Need: credential automation, fewer site visits, centralized device operations
  • Authority: Asset Management, Property Ops, Development, Finance
  • Trigger: new build, renovation, portfolio standardization, unit turnover
ICP C · ENTERPRISE

Manufacturing + high-security facilities

Industrial, technology and other facilities where identity, uptime and auditability matter.

  • Need: resilient access, biometrics, video validation, integration with enterprise identity
  • Authority: Security, Facilities, IT, Risk
  • Trigger: expansion, audit issue, new security leader, legacy platform replacement

BANT → MEDDPICC

Fast filter first. Enterprise rigor second.

BANT answers whether there is a credible deal. MEDDPICC answers whether the organization can actually buy, implement and defend the decision internally.

BANTBudget · Authority · Need · Timing

If one is unknown, identify the evidence required. If all four are weak, do not force an enterprise motion.

MEDDPICC · VALUEMetrics · Pain · Champion

Define the operational or financial outcome and the person who benefits enough to move the deal.

MEDDPICC · DECISIONEconomic buyer · Criteria · Process

Map who approves, what technical and commercial proof they require, and in what order.

MEDDPICC · CLOSEPaper process · Competition

Security review, procurement, MSA, privacy, partner agreement and the real alternative to Xthings.
