NEGOTIATION

Trade scope and commitment, not rate integrity.

The BDM coordinates a three-party commercial dynamic: client budget pressure, Oracle software economics and Baker Tilly delivery economics.

BUYER ASKS FOR SERVICES DISCOUNTTrade scope, not blind rate cuts

Client owns data cleansing, defer secondary integrations, reduce historical migration or move work into Phase 2.

BUYER NEEDS MORE FLEXIBILITYGet a commitment back

Signature date, deposit, executive sponsor cadence, reference participation or narrower scope.

SOFTWARE PRICE IS THE BLOCKERCoordinate with Oracle

Let the software and services teams solve the total economics together while keeping implementation deliverability intact.

Pricing guardrail: actual discount authority, margin floors, Oracle pricing and TMA economics are internal facts. The site demonstrates the give-get principle, not claimed approval thresholds.
FORECAST TRUTH

If the evidence is not in CRM, leadership cannot staff against it.

Commit should mean the economic buyer, decision path, paper process, target date and next mutual action are evidenced. "Customer likes us" is not forecast data.

COMMIT

PIPELINE

Worth working, not worth staffing as near-term revenue.

DISQUALIFY

Protect capacity

No forcing event, no economic buyer, no process, poor fit or no credible change case.
