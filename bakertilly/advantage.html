BATTLECARD LAB

Shape the decision criteria around implementation risk.

These are positioning hypotheses, not claims that every competitor behaves the same way. The goal is to understand the buyer's concern and make Baker Tilly's proof relevant.

WIN CONDITION

Implementation confidence + finance-process redesign

When the buyer fears implementation complexity, position Baker Tilly as the team that can connect system design to accounting, controls and industry process decisions.

  • Bring an industry / accounting SME into executive discovery
  • Use relevant Baker Tilly case proof instead of generic certification counts
  • Keep Oracle software paper and Baker Tilly SOW synchronized so services never delay the license
DO NOT

Attack Oracle's own services team

The Oracle AE is a channel partner, not an enemy. Win by making the joint pursuit safer and faster for the customer and the AE.

WIN CONDITION

Broader client context + credible executive bench

In a competitive NetSuite partner bake-off, the strongest differentiator is often the ability to bring finance, industry and transaction context into one pursuit.

  • Map the buyer's decision scorecard explicitly
  • Use fixed scope / governance proof where supported
  • Equip the champion with Baker Tilly references they can sell internally
DO NOT

Assume brand wins the deal

If a smaller VAR has better references, faster scoping or tighter executive access, Baker Tilly still loses. Earn the win in the pursuit.

WIN CONDITION

Operational complexity beyond finance-only needs

NetSuite becomes stronger when inventory, WMS, manufacturing, multi-entity operations or broader platform consolidation matter in addition to core financials.

  • Quantify complexity, not company size alone
  • Show future-state transaction flows
  • Keep Sage Intacct credible when the buyer truly has lighter requirements
DO NOT

Force NetSuite into a simpler finance use case

Baker Tilly also works with Sage Intacct. That can strengthen credibility if the team demonstrates objective fit rather than platform dogma.

WIN CONDITION

Unified cloud ERP + implementation path

For legacy Dynamics GP/NAV customers, use the forced modernization event to compare future operating model, migration risk, multi-entity needs and total implementation burden.

  • Lead with end-of-life / modernization timing
  • Compare transaction flows and integration requirements
  • Build a five-year TCO and implementation-risk view
DO NOT

Turn it into a feature checklist

The buyer is choosing a future finance operating model, not just replacing an old application.
