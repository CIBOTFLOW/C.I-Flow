FIND THE FORCING EVENT

Prospect the business change, not the ERP category.

The best outbound account is not "a $75M company that might need NetSuite." It is a company where a capital event, finance leadership change, acquisition, system deadline or operational complexity creates a credible reason to change now.

ACCOUNT IQ

Score evidence before spending seller or SC time.

This working model separates fit from timing. A famous company with a new CFO can still be a bad NetSuite target if scale, existing ERP or complexity point elsewhere.

ICP FIT · 30Can NetSuite plausibly fit?

Revenue band, headcount, vertical, ownership, platform stage and likely ERP class.

TRIGGER · 30Why now?

M&A, PE investment, funding, new CFO, ERP sunset, audit/control event or international growth.

COMPLEXITY · 25Is there enough pain?

Multi-entity, inventory, subscription revenue, WMS, manufacturing, global tax or audit requirements.

REACHABILITY · 15Can Baker Tilly get in?

Public finance leader, geographic presence, Oracle overlap, firm relationship or sponsor access.

SIGNAL-BASED REACHOUTS

The message should prove homework before it asks for time.

Current sales commentary is converging on a useful point: methodology is infrastructure. The actual outreach still has to demonstrate curiosity about how the prospect's business changed.

PE / CARVE-OUT

Lead with the separation clock

"What is the deadline for finance systems to stand alone from the seller, and what happens financially if that date slips?"

NEW CFO

Lead with the first 90 days

"Which finance process or system is creating the most drag on the operating model you inherited?"

FUNDING / IPO PATH

Lead with controls + scale

"What has to become audit-ready before the next capital event: close, procurement, revenue recognition or multi-entity reporting?"

ERP SUNSET

Lead with deadline risk

"What is your cutover deadline, and which integrations or finance processes make the migration difficult to defer?"
