TURN TRUST INTO ACCESS

The internal partner should not have to become an ERP salesperson.

The referral engine works when Baker Tilly partners can recognize a signal, ask two or three credible questions, and introduce the NetSuite team without risking the client relationship they have spent years building.

PRACTICE SIGNAL MAP

Each Baker Tilly practice sees a different ERP buying trigger first.

The BDM's job is to translate those practice-specific observations into a simple referral motion, then do the selling and scoping work after the introduction.

WHAT THEY SEECarve-outs, add-ons, QofE and TSA deadlines
  • Parent-company systems need to separate after a transaction
  • Acquired entities run different charts of accounts or inventory systems
  • PE operating teams need consolidated reporting quickly
THE REFERRAL ASKDo not ask them to pitch NetSuite.

"When a carve-out or add-on cannot operate cleanly on the current finance stack, introduce me to the CFO / operating partner. I will qualify the systems path, pull in our delivery team and keep you informed."

WHAT THEY SEEClose friction, control gaps and spreadsheet dependence
  • Month-end close takes 15-20+ days
  • Manual consolidations across entities
  • ASC 606 / approval / segregation-of-duties issues
THE REFERRAL ASKTurn an audit observation into a systems conversation safely.

"If the client is repeatedly spending audit hours on manual reconciliation or weak controls, let me determine whether the root cause is process, ERP or both before anyone recommends a platform."

WHAT THEY SEEInternational expansion and entity complexity
  • New EMEA/APAC entities
  • Multi-currency and intercompany friction
  • VAT, statutory reporting and transfer-pricing data fragmentation
THE REFERRAL ASKUse tax complexity as the timing signal.

"If finance is maintaining separate ledgers or spreadsheets just to support new entities, introduce me to the finance owner. I will validate whether OneWorld / a cloud ERP is appropriate."

WHAT THEY SEEFinance transformation before software selection
  • Interim CFO / Controller identifies process bottlenecks
  • Cash, reporting and planning are disconnected
  • Growth requires headcount because systems do not scale
THE REFERRAL ASKStart with the operating model.

"Where the advisory team sees finance work that should disappear through automation, bring me in to qualify the technology business case and mobilize the right ERP specialists."

REFERRAL FLYWHEEL

Make the warm introduction a repeatable operating motion.

Internal referrals become predictable when the firm has a low-friction cadence, visible credit and a feedback loop back to the originating partner.

1Brief the practice

15-minute industry-team slot: what to notice, what to ask, what not to promise.

2Spot the trigger

M&A, close pain, audit finding, new entity, CFO mandate or system ceiling.

3Make one intro

Originating partner sends a short warm handoff. No ERP expertise required.

4BDM qualifies

Business pain, buyer, process, timing, fit, independence and Oracle alignment.

5Close the loop

Partner sees stage, outcome and attribution so they trust the channel enough to repeat it.

THE INTERNAL ONE-PAGER

Three questions are enough to create the right introduction.

The partner's job is signal recognition, not product selling.

ERP SIGNAL CHECK

Is this client becoming too complex for the way finance operates today?

If the answer to any question below is "yes," send a warm intro. The NetSuite team will handle qualification, demo, proposal and SOW.

OWNERBDM + practice partner
QUESTION 1Are acquisitions, new entities or international growth making consolidated reporting materially harder?
QUESTION 2Is the finance team adding headcount or spreadsheets just to close, bill, buy or reconcile?
QUESTION 3Is there a hard deadline: audit issue, TSA, year-end cutover, ERP sunset, IPO or board mandate?
INDEPENDENCE + TRUST

Protect the client relationship before pursuing the opportunity.

Professional-services selling has a constraint technology sellers do not: the commercial path must respect independence, conflicts and the originating partner's client relationship.

CHECK FIRSTIndependence / conflict status

Audit and attest relationships can restrict permissible advisory or implementation activities. Route the opportunity through Baker Tilly's actual independence and risk process before promising scope.

CLIENT PROTECTIONKeep the originating partner informed

Document who owns the relationship, what the client was told, and the next step. The BDM should create confidence that an introduction strengthens the relationship rather than hijacking it.

Do not improvise regulatory boundaries: the work sample intentionally stops at "check and route." Actual independence conclusions belong to Baker Tilly's Quality / Risk professionals.
