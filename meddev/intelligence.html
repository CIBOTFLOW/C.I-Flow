Scientists and algorithms, but no medical software/SQA depth.

4 · PRODUCTArchitecture shift

prototype to production, hardware freeze, cloud/IoT, RUO to clinical.

5 · MANDATEExternal deadline

MDR transition, AI guidance, change submission, annual report.
