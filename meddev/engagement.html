HOW I WOULD WORK THE MARKET

Gold gets personal attention. Silver gets disciplined scale.

Both tiers come from the same ICP and qualification standard. The difference is how much research, relationship-building and manual effort the account earns.

GOLD LIST

Top Account IQ + strongest buying path

Named accounts with the best combination of fit, urgency, Lead IQ and strategic upside.

  • Phone call first where possible
  • LinkedIn connection + message
  • Personalized email tied to a real signal
  • Offer coffee/lunch when local
  • 10-minute intro or 30-60 minute technical overview
  • Executive roundtable / customer reference / event invitation
SILVER LIST

Strong fit, broader scale

The next layer of the account universe, worked systematically while engagement promotes the best leads.

  • Segmented email sequences
  • Scaled LinkedIn outreach
  • Calls concentrated on best Account IQ / Lead IQ
  • 10-12 total touches
  • Webinars and local events
  • Promote into high-touch treatment when timing changes

CAMPAIGN DEVELOPMENT

Use current customers to make outreach sharper.

The fastest way to improve messaging is to understand how real MedDev relationships began and expanded.

CHAMPIONS

Which titles open doors?

Map the introductory contact, final champion, technical approver and economic buyer across current customers.

LANDING JOB

What gets bought first?

Gap analysis, V&V, QMS, cyber, software development or staff augmentation.

PROOF

Package the right case study.

Turn outcomes into short proof by segment, pain, regulator and buyer. Identify missing case studies.

REFERENCES

Use customer trust.

Offer selected reference conversations when the account is qualified enough to justify them.

FIELD + COMMUNITY

LOCAL MEETINGS

Coffee / lunch

EXECUTIVE ROUNDTABLE

INDUSTRY EVENTS

MD&M West, AdvaMed, RAPS and other relevant events should start from an account list, not badge scans.
