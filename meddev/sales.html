QUALIFICATION

Use BANT for reality. Use MEDDPICC for control.

The qualification depth should match the deal, but budget, authority, need and timing must become explicit early.

BANT

Is there a real project?

  • Budget: targeted remediation vs full turnkey scope
  • Authority: CTO / VP R&D, RA/QA, CEO / board
  • Need: submission, V&V capacity, audit gap or commercialization
  • Timing: typically a 6-12 month market or submission target
MEDDPICC

Can we control the decision?

  • Metrics: months saved, hiring avoided, traceability, submission risk
  • Economic buyer: CEO / VP R&D budget owner
  • Criteria: IEC 62304, ISO 13485, speed, algorithm depth
  • Process: technical review → gap assessment → proposal → legal/IP → signoff
  • Champion: person living the compliance or delivery problem
DEAL COMPRESSION

Know what is really blocking the signature.

Price pressure, technical uncertainty and internal delay are different problems and need different responses.

CHAMPION

Use internal urgency.

Give the champion the technical proof, scope and business case needed to sell internally.

DECISION MAKER

Get direct access.

Do not let critical scope, timeline or risk decisions remain second-hand.

ECONOMIC BUYER

Protect value.

Separate true budget constraints from procurement tactics and quantify the cost of delay.

PROCUREMENT

Parallelize the close.

Start NDA, IP, security, MSA and PO work early enough that paperwork does not become the project bottleneck.
