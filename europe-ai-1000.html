WHY THIS SPLIT

Two different budgets inside the same enterprise.

Enterprise Agents covers internal work. Customer Service covers external service operations. That separation reduces vendor overlap while preserving the ability to create a second opportunity from the same relationship.

ONE ENTERPRISE RELATIONSHIP

Same account. Different problem owners.

A COO or CIO conversation can expose both internal workflow friction and customer-service volume without forcing every problem into one product.
