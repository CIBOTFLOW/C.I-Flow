THE PREMISE

Act as the European revenue layer, not a generic lead-gen agency.

The same account universe can support multiple non-competing AI solutions. C.I Flow retains the account intelligence and territory discipline while each vendor chooses how much of the sales cycle it wants us to own.

01

Territory + account intelligence

Define the ICP, maintain the 1,000-account universe, map use cases, buying signals, stakeholders and timing.

02

Outbound + appointment setting

Account research, calling, email, LinkedIn, personalized messaging and qualified first meetings.

03

Discovery + qualification

Diagnose the workflow, economic value, decision process, implementation constraints and next commercial step.

04

Three closing lanes

Vendor close: C.I Flow sources and qualifies. Co-sell: we stay active with the vendor. Full-cycle: Connor owns the commercial process through signature.

05

Channel + reseller

Once a product proves repeatable demand, move from sourced commission into registered deals, reseller margin and recurring economics.

06

Expansion + adoption

Coordinate implementation, training and expansion opportunities so a first deployment can become a broader account.
