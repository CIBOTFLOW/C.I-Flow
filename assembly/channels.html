LOCAL DENSITY IS A GROWTH ADVANTAGE

Turn the Bay Area into a network, not a list of isolated prospects.

HOA management is local, referral-heavy and trust-driven. The highest-leverage motion combines targeted outbound with neighborhood density, professional referral partners, existing boards and small in-person events.

CHANNEL MIX

Build four ways into the same boardroom.

The mix below comes from your prep and should be measured by qualified ARR, not just lead count.

40% TARGET OUTBOUND

Gold / Silver lists

Public records, local HOA directories, incumbent reviews and board-role research produce account-specific outreach.

25% INBOUND

Web + Atlas interest

Move high-intent board inquiries quickly into audit and discovery rather than generic demo scheduling.

20% REFERRAL

Trusted intermediaries

Brokers, HOA attorneys, escrow/title officers, reserve-study firms, developers and existing board members.

15% FIELD

Events + territory days

CAI/ECHO events, treasurer dinners, coffee meetings, property walks and local board presentations.

MICRO-TERRITORY FIELD DAY

Stack trust-building activity inside a five-mile radius.

The prep's drive-time optimization is exactly right for a field BD role. One neighborhood should produce multiple prospect and partner interactions in the same day.

REFERRAL ENGINE

Own the people who hear “we hate our HOA manager” first.

Referral partners can surface dissatisfaction months before a formal management RFP appears.

HOA ATTORNEYS

Governance + dispute signal

Often see board dysfunction, covenant enforcement, contract issues and management breakdown before anyone else.

RESERVE STUDY FIRMS

Financial + capital signal

See underfunding, deferred maintenance and boards approaching difficult capital decisions.

BROKERS / REALTORS

Reputation signal

See communities where poor management is hurting transactions, disclosure experience or buyer confidence.

ESCROW / TITLE

Transaction friction

See slow document delivery, incomplete records and recurring management-process problems during transfers.

Compliance before incentives: any paid referral structure should be reviewed by Assembly Legal against California real-estate, HOA-management and professional-referral rules before launch. The strategic value is the channel, not a specific unverified bounty amount.
ENGAGEMENT CADENCE

Gold gets field time. Silver earns it through intent.

Different investment levels, not permanent labels. A Silver account can immediately become high-touch when timing changes.

GOLD · 21-DAY HIGH-TOUCH

Personal, local, board-specific.

  • Day 1: personalized email / LinkedIn with one community-specific observation
  • Day 3: direct call to President or Treasurer
  • Day 7: useful governance / operations resource and coffee invitation
  • Day 12: call + invitation to Treasurer roundtable or dinner
  • Day 18: relevant local transition story / proof
  • Day 21: ask for 15-minute operational audit
SILVER · 14-DAY SCALED

Relevant enough to earn a response.

  • Day 1: email around a specific management problem
  • Day 4: LinkedIn connection
  • Day 8: invite to webinar / local education event
  • Day 14: follow-up with Atlas / platform proof
  • Promote to high-touch immediately on engagement or trigger event
