MONDAY MORNING PIPELINE

Start with the opportunities already earned, then add net-new.

Growth should combine disciplined reactivation of former opportunities and old leads with public-data prospecting for new communities. The site shows the operating process, not an upload utility or invented CRM history.

CRM REACTIVATION

Old pipeline is a channel, not an archive.

On day one I would pull every former lead and opportunity, preserve the original context, rescore fit and timing, then return the highest-value records to an active next-action queue.

01Closed Lost

Separate true competitive loss from timing, pricing, incumbent contract, board politics and no decision.

02Stalled Opportunity

Anything that reached discovery, audit, proposal or board discussion but lost momentum.

03No Decision

Boards that did not reject Assembly, they simply stayed with the status quo.

04Former Lead

Inbound, referral or outbound contacts that never became a qualified opportunity.

05Nurture

Good-fit community with bad timing. Re-score against current board, contract and capital triggers.

06Former Customer / Referral

Where appropriate, mine prior relationships for introductions, market intelligence and re-entry paths.

No fake CRM history: this work sample does not invent past Assembly opportunities. On day one, I would work the real CRM inside Assembly's existing system and preserve every prior note, contact, loss reason and board interaction.
REACTIVATION OPERATING LANE

Turn old pipeline into a weekly revenue motion.

The CRM itself remains the system of record. The job is to create a disciplined review process that brings viable records back to life without adding another tool.

01 · SEGMENTWhy did it stop?

Separate timing, incumbent contract, budget, board politics, competitor loss, bad fit and true disqualification.

02 · RE-SCOREWhat changed?

Refresh Account IQ, board leadership, management contract timing, capital projects and current intent.

03 · PRIORITIZEReactivate now vs nurture

Only return records to active pipeline when there is a reason to believe timing or fit has materially improved.

04 · ASSIGNOne owner, one next action

Every reactivated record gets a dated action, contact strategy and clear exit criterion.

05 · LEARNFeed the loss data back

Use recurring loss reasons to improve targeting, pricing, messaging and transition planning.
